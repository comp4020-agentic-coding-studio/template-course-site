Replace this page. A topic is the smallest unit of course content that is worth
linking to: one concept, one tool, or one activity, explained once. Everything
else in the site --- lectures, sessions, assessment briefs --- links here rather
than restating it, so a correction lands in one place.

## What belongs on a topic page

The idea, what it is for, and the smallest worked example that makes it land.
Aim for a page a student can read in a few minutes and come back to in week 10.

## What does not

Schedule information, marking rules, and anything that changes between
offerings. Those have their own pages, and duplicating them here is how two
pages start disagreeing.

## Headings are addresses

Other pages deep-link into headings on this one, so renaming a heading breaks
those links while demoting it (h2 to h3) does not. Treat the wording as part of
the interface.
