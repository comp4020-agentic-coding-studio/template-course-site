A topic covers one idea and owns it. Lectures and sessions link here rather than
re-explaining, so a correction lands in one place. The week badges are derived
from the content graph: a lecture that declares a topic in its `related:`
frontmatter puts its week on that topic's card, and nothing restates the
schedule.
