Every course website answers the same three questions on its front page: what is
this, is it for me, and what do I do next. Answer them here, in that order, and
put everything else one click away.

## What you will do

Say what a student spends their time on --- the making, reading, arguing or
measuring that fills the weeks. Name the artefact they walk out with.

## Who it is for

Say who this is aimed at and what they need to already know. Be specific enough
that someone can rule themselves in or out without emailing you.
