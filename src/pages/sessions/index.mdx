What this slot is called is a design decision, not a default. Lab, tute,
workshop, studio, seminar, crit --- each name sets an expectation about who
talks and who watches, so pick the one that describes what actually happens and
use it everywhere. The collection ships as `sessions`; renaming it is a
five-file change, and `CLAUDE.md` walks through it.
